Open the existing `Jkoreaboi/luvbird` project. The Next.js landing page stays at the repository root, and the DearBird app and API stay in `legacy-mvp/`.

Read `CURSOR_START_HERE.md` before making changes. The marketing site, the product baseline, and the decision not to move the trees into `apps/` are already recorded there. A separate Webild visual concept is reference only; its code is not in this repository.

Do not assume the landing page is the whole product. Do not rewrite features from scratch before checking whether they already exist in `legacy-mvp/`. Do not delete the landing page, and do not perform a monorepo migration until package scripts, imports, environment variables, tests, and deployment assumptions are checked again.

Product rules that already exist in the API:

- A letter travels for 24 hours. Production refuses any other duration.
- One traveling letter per sender and recipient. A retry of the same send is allowed. The other person can still send their own letter.
- Discovery filters country, language, interest, and purpose on the server before returning at most 100 people.
- Three requests a day, three active letter connections, three letters a day.
- No read receipts, likes, followers, or precise location.

Do not present physical mail, store release, payments, ads, or AI matching as shipped. Do not add fake accounts.
